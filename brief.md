# Working brief, revised 5 September 2026

This brief incorporates the author's clarification in chats/chat.md and the
subsequent editorial exchange. Earlier research remains in research.md and
sources.md as dated provenance.

## Question and intended reading

What remains owed when an institution no longer needs the work around which it
organized a life? The pigeon history must answer that question on its own terms
while allowing a reader to recognize the contemporary problem of workers in an
AI economy. Keep that second reading implicit: no AI references, forecasts or
explicit human–pest analogy in the manuscript. Do not reduce living animals to
symbols for people.

Keep the title Workers Without Work and its historical subtitle. End with
“Usefulness can end before membership does.” Earn the sentence by distinguishing
a replacement use from a claim that survives the absence of any useful task.

## Argument

Skinner's retained birds introduce competence and care that outlast a program.
The Reuter-Geller contract and manure economy show what a function holds
together. Distributed decommissioning describes the uneven withdrawal of those
arrangements; it is not a reconstructed genealogy of every city flock.

Army sales, the horse-disposal market and retirement statutes distinguish the
power to dispose of an animal from the acceptance of continuing duties. These
cases illustrate choices, not a predictive typology. Remove the four-cell table
and let the cases carry the distinction.

Control literature shows how a familiar capacity becomes an objection to an
animal's presence. Built space makes that presence difficult to wish away.
Ecology and real harms constrain management without deciding the obligation.

The last section distinguishes payment for past service from a standing claim
grounded in residence and relationships. It also distinguishes finding another
job from accepting responsibility when no such job exists. A managed loft is a
brief example to evaluate, not the argument's culmination.

## Evidence and limits

Claims are bound in claims.yaml, with inspection scope in source-checks.md.
Historical laws are discussed as enacted. The Barcelona intervention combined
education and culling and permits movement effects. Pimentel's control-cost
extrapolation is not a national survey of actual losses. Documentary silence
about retirement in Reuter's contract is not proof of abandonment.

Membership is used in an expressly limited normative sense: a standing claim
to consideration in decisions about a shared place. It is not an assertion of
existing citizenship or unrestricted permission to occupy any site. The ethical
premise is additional to the history, and historical contribution alone cannot
allocate every present duty. The paper does not forecast labour-market change
or establish the efficacy of a contemporary pigeon-management program.
