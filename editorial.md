## Contribution and authorial intention

The question is what remains owed after an institution ceases to need a worker's
function. Pigeons supply a historical case with its own evidentiary demands.
The author wants the implication for workers in the AI era to remain unspoken.
The whole of chats/chat.md was read after this clarification.

Retain the title and historical subtitle. Use “Usefulness can end before
membership does” as the last sentence, with no appended explanation. The final
section must earn it through a distinction between a replacement use and a
continuing claim. Do not turn the birds into an allegorical prop.

## Material preserved and removed

Preserve Skinner's retained birds, the manure economy, the Reuter-Geller contract,
the Army sale, the horse-disposal market, the handbook's homing problem and the
qualified Barcelona finding. These provide concrete institutional decisions and
limits on the argument.

The archaeological capacity estimate and Darwin excursus were removed to shorten
the catalogue of pigeon uses. The four-cell table was replaced by the comparative
narrative. The explicit analogy with anti-homeless spikes was removed so the
wider implication can remain latent. Source-check history for these passages is
retained separately; their references are absent from the current bibliography.

## Reverse outline and structure

Original: project cancellation; five pigeon uses; distributed withdrawal;
deterministic four-cell typology; inversion of capacities; pest classification;
nonspace; municipal obligations.

First rewrite corrected the causal overreach but still culminated in municipal
pigeon management. The author identified this as missing the intended centre.

Current: a program ends before competence; a function organizes a life; custody
can end under different settlements of obligation; a capacity becomes a nuisance;
a population remains spatially present; a claim survives without another job.
The return to Skinner near the end distinguishes continued usefulness from care
when no further task is available.

## Strongest rivals and inferential decisions

Ecology, markets and reproduction help explain population persistence. The horse
comparison does not isolate ownership as its cause. No two-variable law is
asserted. A transferred animal need not be well cared for; protection need not
exclude every harmful intervention.

The normative rival is that duties should end with ownership, or extend only as
repayment for demonstrated past service. The conclusion instead argues for
consideration grounded in residence and continuing relationships. Membership is
defined narrowly and openly as a normative proposal, not a fact established by
the historical cases or a claim of existing legal citizenship.

A new useful task may sustain care but leaves recognition conditional on demand.
The paper now addresses that difference rather than assuming that a better
pigeon-management device resolves the argument.

## Source and wording decisions

- Dog adoption was conditional; the horse enactment included management and
  destruction. The CHIMP Act established lifetime care within stated conditions.
  These discussions concern the original enactments, not every current rule.
- Skinner's thirty retained birds are on p. 34 and the six-year test on p. 36.
- The horse figure is a firm's claimed profit, not a universal carcass price.
- The Reuter-Geller contract replaces an inaccessible secondary book. Its whole
  transcription was reread; no retirement clause does not mean proven neglect.
- Pimentel's estimate extrapolates control expenditure as a damage proxy.
- Barcelona combined treatments, was not a randomized isolation of them, and
  permits movement effects. The text retains those qualifications.
- A loft remains an unevaluated proposal here. Giunchi's evidence assessment is
  dated to 2012, not presented as a current literature consensus.
- Twenty-two cited works have stable CSL identities. The ledger separates source
  claims, definitions, interpretations and ethical premises.
- Limited use of publisher/author synopses is disclosed in source-checks.md. No
  whole-book reading is claimed. The author's Zoopolis synopsis was reread for
  the membership revision; the manuscript's own ethical premise is identified.
- AI, automation forecasts and direct human–pest comparisons remain outside the
  paper. The final sentence is plain prose, not a highlighted slogan.
