This record concerns the revised manuscript, not every work in the original
research bibliography. `references.json` supplies citation identities;
`claims.yaml` binds the central source claims and the paper's own inferences.
A checked passage is not a claim that the whole source has been read. Publisher
or author synopses are identified below where they suffice for a narrow framing
claim. Historical laws are discussed as enacted, not as current legal advice.

## Changes to the earlier research record

The Reuter-Geller contract replaces Read's inaccessible book for the route example.
The 1851 termination date is no longer needed. The 1939 patent digression was
removed: it added little to the argument, and the full patent could not be
retrieved in this revision. The later membership-focused revision also removed
the archaeological capacity estimate, Darwin's breed comparison and the explicit
comparison with anti-homeless architecture. Their checks are retained below as
research history, not citations required by the current argument.
The compact bibliography contains only works used in the current manuscript.
Earlier source and research notes remain as dated records, including stronger
claims superseded by this revision.

## Scope of what remains open

This paper does not establish loft efficacy today, reconstruct the origins of a
particular urban flock, estimate institutional effects separately from ecology,
or assess every animal's welfare after transfer. These are substantive limits,
not missing simulation checks. Further work on them would require new research.
